# Longflow orchestrator

**Problem:** complex delivery can lose intent between planning, implementation, and review.

**Why useful:** this entry point coordinates the complete Longflow with explicit gates and durable evidence.

**Scope:** substantial intent-to-delivery work; it is heavier than a direct small fix and depends on the companion Longflow skills.

**Use:** invoke `longflow-orchestrator` after selecting and reviewing the Longflow pack. Follow [installation guidance](../../../../INSTALL.md).
