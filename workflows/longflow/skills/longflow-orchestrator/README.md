# Longflow orchestrator

**Problem:** complex delivery can lose intent between planning, implementation, and review.

**Why useful:** this entry point captures intent, calibrates how much process the task deserves (possibly none), and runs only that subset with promise-shaped gates and durable evidence.

**Scope:** intent-to-delivery work at any size — its first decision is the tier, and "just build it" is a legitimate outcome. Depends on the companion Longflow skills at T2+.

**Use:** invoke `longflow-orchestrator` after selecting and reviewing the Longflow pack. Follow [installation guidance](../../../../INSTALL.md).
